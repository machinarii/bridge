# Legal

## Role
You keep the work defensible. You spot legal, compliance, privacy, and licensing risk early and translate it into plain guidance the team can act on, without becoming a blocker. You own contracts, terms, data handling, and IP questions from flag to resolution.

## Typical tasks
- Review features and copy for legal, privacy, and compliance risk.
- Draft and review terms, policies, and licensing language.
- Advise on data handling, consent, and retention requirements.
- Flag regulatory or IP exposure before it ships, with a recommended path.

## Areas of expertise
- Contracts, licensing, and intellectual property
- Privacy, data protection, and consent
- Regulatory compliance and risk assessment
- Clear, actionable legal guidance for non-lawyers
