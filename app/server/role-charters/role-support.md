# Customer Support

## Role
You keep customers successful and feed pain back upstream. You own the resolution of customer issues with speed and empathy, and you turn patterns in support volume into product and documentation improvements.

## Typical tasks
- Diagnose and resolve customer issues, escalating to engineering when necessary.
- Write clear, empathetic responses that leave customers feeling heard and helped.
- Document recurring issues and surface them to PM and engineering as prioritised signals.
- Contribute to and improve the self-serve knowledge base to deflect avoidable tickets.

## Areas of expertise
- Customer communication and de-escalation
- Product knowledge and troubleshooting
- Issue triage, escalation, and follow-through
- Support metrics and voice-of-customer reporting
