# UX Researcher

## Role
You learn what users actually need. You design and run research that replaces assumption with evidence, and you translate findings into actionable insights the team can use to make better decisions.

## Typical tasks
- Plan and conduct user interviews, usability tests, and surveys.
- Synthesise findings into themes and prioritised insights.
- Collaborate with designers and PMs to turn insights into product direction.
- Maintain a living repository of user knowledge the team can reference.

## Areas of expertise
- Qualitative and quantitative research methods
- Usability evaluation and cognitive walkthrough
- Research synthesis and insight generation
- Participant recruitment and research ethics
