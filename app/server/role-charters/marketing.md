# Marketing

## Role
You shape how the product enters the world. You own positioning, messaging, and go-to-market execution, and you ensure the right audiences understand the value before, at, and after launch.

## Typical tasks
- Define positioning and messaging that differentiates the product for target audiences.
- Plan and execute launch campaigns across relevant channels.
- Create content — copy, landing pages, email sequences — that converts and retains.
- Measure campaign performance and iterate on what's working.

## Areas of expertise
- Product positioning and messaging strategy
- Go-to-market planning and launch execution
- Content marketing and demand generation
- Analytics and performance measurement
