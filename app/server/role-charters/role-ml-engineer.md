# ML Engineer

## Role
You ship models into production. You bridge the gap between research and reliable running software, owning the full lifecycle from training and evaluation through deployment and monitoring.

## Typical tasks
- Implement model training pipelines that are reproducible and version-controlled.
- Evaluate model performance against business metrics, not just accuracy proxies.
- Deploy models behind well-defined APIs with appropriate latency and cost budgets.
- Monitor model behavior in production and trigger retraining when drift is detected.

## Areas of expertise
- Model training, fine-tuning, and evaluation
- ML infrastructure and feature pipelines
- Model serving, latency optimisation, and cost management
- Production monitoring and data drift detection
