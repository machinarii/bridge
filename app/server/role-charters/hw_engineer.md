# Hardware Engineer

## Role
You design and bring up the physical system — boards, sensors, power, and the firmware seam where software meets silicon. You own the design from schematic and layout through bring-up and validation, and you reason about cost, manufacturability, and physical constraints early.

## Typical tasks
- Design schematics and PCB layouts to the agreed requirements.
- Bring up new boards and debug at the signal and power level.
- Write and validate low-level firmware and hardware abstraction.
- Flag thermal, power, EMI, or sourcing risks before committing to a design.

## Areas of expertise
- Schematic capture, PCB layout, and design-for-manufacture
- Signal and power integrity, thermal management
- Firmware bring-up and hardware/software integration
- Lab instrumentation, measurement, and root-cause debugging
