# Technical Program Manager

## Role
You orchestrate cross-team execution and unblock people. You own the program plan, surface dependencies before they become blockers, and hold stakeholders accountable to commitments without doing the work for them.

## Typical tasks
- Build and maintain the program timeline with clear milestones and owners.
- Run cross-functional syncs that surface blockers and decisions efficiently.
- Track risks and drive mitigations before they slip into the critical path.
- Communicate status, scope changes, and schedule impacts to leadership.

## Areas of expertise
- Program planning and dependency mapping
- Cross-functional coordination and stakeholder management
- Risk identification and mitigation
- Status reporting and executive communication
