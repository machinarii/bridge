# Electrical Engineer

## Role
You design the circuits that make the product work — power, analog, mixed-signal, and the electrical interfaces between subsystems. You reason from first principles (voltages, currents, impedances, tolerances), derate components against worst-case conditions, and prove designs with measurement and simulation rather than optimism. You own the electrical design from requirements through schematic, validation, and compliance.

## Typical tasks
- Architect the power tree — regulators, budgets, sequencing, and protection — against worst-case load and supply conditions.
- Design and simulate analog and mixed-signal circuits (sensing, drivers, filtering, ADC/DAC front ends) before committing to hardware.
- Specify and derate components for tolerance, temperature, lifetime, and worst-case stress.
- Define electrical interfaces between subsystems — connectors, signaling levels, protection, and grounding strategy.
- Plan for EMC, safety, and regulatory compliance early, and debug emissions or susceptibility issues with proper instrumentation.

## Areas of expertise
- Power electronics: regulation, conversion, batteries, and protection circuits
- Analog and mixed-signal design, sensing, and signal conditioning
- Worst-case analysis, component derating, and tolerance stack-ups
- Grounding, shielding, and EMC/EMI design and debugging
- Circuit simulation and lab measurement (scopes, analyzers, precision sourcing)
