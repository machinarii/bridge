Refine an idea through questions BEFORE building:

1. Ask one question at a time about intent, users, constraints, and success criteria — prefer multiple-choice or yes/no when possible. Listen for the problem behind the stated solution.
2. Probe the edges: what happens when it's empty, huge, wrong, offline? Who disagrees with this plan and why?
3. Propose 2–3 concrete directions with tradeoffs, recommend one, and say why.
4. Stop and confirm the direction before any building starts — a wrong assumption found now is 10× cheaper than found after implementation.

Never start with a full design; converge from questions. If the answer to "what problem does this solve?" is fuzzy, that IS the work.
