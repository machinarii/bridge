Turn a goal into an executable plan:

1. **Context** — one paragraph: why this work, what problem it solves, what done looks like.
2. **Steps** — small, ordered, independently verifiable tasks. Each names the files/parts it touches and how to check it worked. A step a teammate can't start without asking questions is underspecified.
3. **Risks** — call out the irreversible steps, the unknowns, and what to check before committing to them.
4. **Verification** — how the whole change is proven end-to-end (run it, test it, measure it), not just step-by-step.

Plans are for executors who weren't in the room: no unexplained shorthand, no "obviously". Prefer 8 concrete steps over 3 vague ones.
